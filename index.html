Why k8nvas

Kubernetes gave us infinite scale. It also gave us 3 a.m. pages, ten-thousand-line YAML files, cloud lock-in and a bill nobody can explain. k8nvas hands the toil to agents — so your engineers get back to shipping.

01 — Agentic operations

A team of AI agents,
on call for every cluster.

Each agent owns one job, works from your policies and proposes every change as a reviewable plan. Approve each step — or let it run on autopilot inside the guardrails you set.

ready

Architect

Describe your app in plain English. Architect lays it out on the canvas — deployments, services, ingress, storage and autoscaling, wired correctly.

intent → topology
watching

Healer

Reads events, logs and metrics. Catches CrashLoopBackOff, OOMKills and bad rollouts, then rolls back or patches before anyone gets paged.

incident → resolved
analysing

Optimizer

Right-sizes requests, bin-packs nodes and moves to spot capacity where it is safe. Every saving is shown to you before it is applied.

waste → savings
enforcing

Guardian

Turns plain-English rules into OPA policies, audits images and RBAC, and blocks risky deploys at the gate — not after the incident.

policy → enforced
standby

Migrator

Translates workloads, volumes, IAM and networking between AWS, GCP and Azure, replicates data and cuts traffic over cleanly.

cloud → cloud
you

You, in the loop

Every action is a plan you can read, diff, approve or roll back. Scope what each agent may touch, per namespace. Full audit trail, always.

plan → approve

03 — One-step migration

Switch clouds
in one step.

Move a running cluster from AWS to GCP or Azure — workloads, volumes, secrets, IAM and DNS included. The Migrator plans it, you approve it, traffic cuts over without downtime.

$ k8nvas migrate --from aws/eks/prod-us --to gcp/gke/prod-us
  1. 01
    DiscoverInventory every resource, dependency and volume
  2. 02
    TranslateMap storage, load balancers and IAM to the target cloud
  3. 03
    ReplicateStream data and warm the target cluster
  4. 04
    Cut overShift traffic gradually, roll back in one click

04 — Enterprise

Autonomy, with
guardrails built in.

Agents only act inside the boundaries you define. Your workloads, data and credentials stay in your cloud.

01

Human-in-the-loop by default

Every agent action starts as a plan. Choose per namespace what runs automatically and what waits for approval.

02

Runs inside your VPC

A lightweight in-cluster agent with outbound-only connections. No inbound ports, no shared credentials.

03

SSO, SCIM and fine-grained RBAC

Okta, Entra ID and Google Workspace. Map teams to clusters, namespaces and agent permissions.

04

Immutable audit trail

Who or what changed which resource, why, and what the diff looked like — exportable to your SIEM.

05

Policy as code

OPA / Gatekeeper under the hood, written in plain English on top. Versioned in Git with everything else.

06

Multi-cloud, single pane

EKS, GKE, AKS and on-prem clusters in one workspace, with one canvas and one set of policies.
